# ADD-1 Bayview Pump Station Upgrade

Addendum issue order 1. Read each item with SPEC-01/1.2.

## 1 Planned station outage

Revise SPEC-02/1.2. Replace the stated requirement with the following requirement.

The maximum planned station outage is 4 hours.

The shorter window reflects the owner revised collection-system operating plan. Keep the rollback connection available until permanent service is proved.

## 2 Foundation concrete strength

Revise SPEC-05/2.1. Replace the stated requirement with the following requirement.

The specified foundation concrete strength is 40 MPa.

Submit a revised mix design for the manifold foundation. The change does not alter the required foundation dimensions or the inspection hold point.

## 3 Pump warranty duration

Revise SPEC-09/2.2. Replace the stated requirement with the following requirement.

The pump warranty period is 24 months.

Apply the revised period to the complete pump package. The warranty still begins at station acceptance.
