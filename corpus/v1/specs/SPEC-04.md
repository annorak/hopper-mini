# SPEC-04 Site work and pavement restoration

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Access arrangement

The station access apron remains the delivery route and the owner emergency access route. Use a traffic marshal when equipment reverses through the gate. Keep trench plates flush with the adjoining pavement. Do not store pipe or excavated material within the vehicle turning area.

## 1.2 Topsoil area

The topsoil stripping area is 650 m2. Strip the grassed shoulder only within the restoration boundary. Store reusable topsoil separately from trench spoil and keep weed growth controlled. Reinstate the shoulder with the original topsoil after buried work passes inspection.

## 2.1 Topsoil depth

The design topsoil stripping depth is 0.15 m. The depth is measured below the existing turf surface. Remove roots and unsuitable material from the reusable stockpile. Do not blend aggregate trench spoil into topsoil to make up a shortfall.

## 2.2 East-gate tie-in

The east-gate tie-in finished grade is not established. The engineer of record shall select that grade after the retained road edge is surveyed. Do not infer the grade from the existing gate threshold. Hold the final pavement course at this tie-in until the engineer of record issues the elevation.

## 2.3 Vehicle apron

The vehicle-apron restoration area is 180 m2. Restore the apron over accepted trench backfill with a compacted aggregate base and asphalt surface. Match adjoining crossfall away from the control-room door. Keep utility covers accessible and set them flush with the finished surface.

## 3.1 Walking surface

The walkway restoration uses a slip-resistant concrete finish at the control-room entrance. Keep the walkway separate from the sump discharge point. Reinstate damaged edging before placing the surface. The transition at the door shall allow the owner to roll a maintenance cart through the opening.

## 3.2 Final drainage check

After pavement work, wet the apron and observe the runoff path to the retained catch basin. Correct ponding that blocks the access route or sends water toward the electrical room. Clear sediment from the catch basin before acceptance. Record the drainage check in the restoration inspection report.
