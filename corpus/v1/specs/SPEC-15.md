# SPEC-15 Plumbing, sump, and drainage

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Drainage scope

Provide the valve-room sump pump, condensate drains, washwater hose connection, and local floor-drain repairs. Keep building drainage separate from the pressure-main drainage arrangement. Route sump discharge through a visible inspection point. Do not connect a drain to the wet well without an identified connection detail.

## 1.2 Sump event volume

The design sump pump-out volume per event is 160 L. The volume is the expected liquid removed during a normal drain-down event. Use it for daily drainage planning with the event frequency on the plumbing sheet. It does not establish the sump-pump emergency capacity.

## 2.1 Sump operation

The sump pump uses local level control and a separate high-level alarm contact. Provide a removable pump and a serviceable nonreturn valve. Keep the alarm available when the pump local isolator is open. Identify the sump discharge line along its full exposed route.

## 2.2 Condensate drains

Provide trapped condensate drains with accessible cleanouts at equipment connections. Discharge through the air gap shown on the plumbing sheet. Support the drain so retained condensate cannot run back into the equipment. Prime traps before the ventilation acceptance run.

## 2.3 Washwater backflow device

The washwater service backflow-preventer type is not selected. The engineer of record shall select the device after the utility confirms its connection requirements. No default device type is authorized by this specification. Hold procurement of the backflow assembly until the selection is issued.

## 3.1 Drainage test

Flow clean water through each new drain and observe discharge at the intended outlet. Confirm that cleanout covers remain accessible after equipment installation. Test the sump alarm independently of pump start. Correct leakage and trapped sections before floor restoration closes the route.

## 3.2 Drainage cleaning

Remove debris from retained floor drains affected by construction. Do not flush grout, coating residue, or oil into the sump. Clean the sump after the final equipment washdown. Give the owner a record of the discharge connection and the accepted pump local-control settings.
