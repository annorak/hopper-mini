# SPEC-08 Process piping and joints

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Piping scope

Replace the exposed discharge branches and the common manifold, then connect the new pressure main to the retained downstream spool. Include flanges, supports, drains, and dismantling joints. Keep the existing gravity inlet untouched. All spool dimensions shall come from the contractor field survey before fabrication.

## 1.2 Branch test volume

The pump-branch hydrostatic test volume is 2.4 m3. This allowance includes the branches and the capped manifold section within the branch test boundary. Submit a sketch showing the test caps and vent points. Use clean water and collect the drained test water for controlled disposal.

## 2.1 Discharge route length

The full discharge-route pipe length is 132 m. This length includes the retained downstream portion. Use ductile-iron pipe for the new buried pressure-main run and flanged steel spools within the valve room. Keep material transition joints accessible for inspection before backfill.

## 2.2 Retained connection

Clean and inspect the retained downstream spool before making the tie-in. Verify flange face condition and bolt-hole alignment without forcing the new pipe into place. Protect the open line from debris during the outage. Renew the flange fasteners as part of the tie-in work.

## 2.3 Legacy flange gasket

The gasket elastomer for the retained legacy force-main flange is not selected. The engineer of record shall select it after the owner provides the historical cleaning-chemical record. The new-pipe gasket schedule does not apply to this legacy connection. Do not procure that gasket from an assumed wastewater service classification.

## 3.1 Main test volume

The buried pressure-main hydrostatic test volume is 18 m3. This boundary is separate from the pump-branch test boundary. Fill from the low point and vent trapped air before pressurization. Keep the test record tied to the pipe segment shown on the accepted test sketch.

## 3.2 Flushing and acceptance

Remove construction debris before connecting the new piping to the pumps. Install temporary strainers only where the pump supplier permits them, and remove them after flushing. Inspect each dismantling joint for movement after the first wet run. Mark final flange and tie-rod settings on the assembly record.
