# SPEC-05 Cast-in-place concrete

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Concrete work

Provide the new manifold foundation, pump support plinths, cable-duct encasement, and local slab repairs. Verify sleeves and embedded plates before concrete placement. Keep concrete clear of valve-operating and equipment-removal spaces. The contractor shall coordinate embeds with the approved equipment shop drawings.

## 1.2 Foundation quantity

The manifold foundation concrete volume is 22 m3. This volume excludes the pump support plinths. Place the foundation on inspected bearing material and keep the form interior free of standing water. The placement record shall identify the batch tickets used for the foundation.

## 2.1 Concrete strength

The specified foundation concrete strength is 35 MPa. Submit the proposed mix and trial data before the first structural pour. Do not add water at the site without the supplier recorded authorization. Cure exposed surfaces immediately after the finish can support the curing cover.

## 2.2 Anchors at existing wall

The anchor embedment for the retained masonry wall connection has not been designed. The engineer of record shall set the embedment after reviewing the exposed wall construction. Do not use the new-concrete anchor schedule for this connection. No anchor embedment value for this wall is provided elsewhere in the contract documents.

## 2.3 Construction joints

Locate construction joints away from anchor groups and sleeve clusters. Clean laitance and loose aggregate from the joint before the next placement. Provide a keyed joint at the interface shown in the accepted pour plan. Keep waterstop splices accessible for inspection before the joint closes.

## 3.1 Plinth placement

Cast pump support plinths after the accepted pump base dimensions are available. Roughen the foundation contact surface and clean it before placement. Check anchor alignment with the pump supplier template. Protect grout pockets from debris until the equipment installer accepts them.

## 3.2 Reinforcement reconciliation

The scheduled reinforcing steel quantity is 2800 kg. The first reinforcing steel delivery quantity is 2100 kg. These quantities cover the new foundation and plinths. Reconcile delivery tags against the bar schedule before the final shipment, and retain the tag record with the reinforcement inspection.
