# SPEC-20 Closeout, spares, and final records

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Closeout scope

Deliver the final drawings, accepted operating manuals, spare parts, test records, and warranty contacts before final acceptance. Keep the equipment tag consistent across every record. The closeout package shall identify remaining owner actions without treating unfinished contractor work as an owner maintenance task.

## 1.2 Record drawings

Update pipe routes, cable routes, concealed joints, final equipment tags, and changed connections on the record drawings. Show field dimensions for buried tie-ins before backfill conceals them. Include the survey reference used for the final route. Record drawings shall show installed work rather than unaccepted proposed revisions.

## 2.1 Spare-part register

List each delivered spare by equipment tag, manufacturer part number, quantity, and storage requirement. Distinguish consumable media from repair assemblies. The owner shall sign the inventory at physical handover. A purchase order is not evidence that a spare has been delivered.

## 2.2 Final electronic record

Provide searchable manuals and native controller and drive configuration files in the owner folder structure. Include a manifest that relates each file to the installed equipment or test. Check that the owner can open the delivered files without a contractor account. Transfer credentials by the owner approved secure method.

## 2.3 Owner fiber provision

The minimum bend radius for the future owner fiber cable is not established. The engineer of record shall determine that radius after the owner selects the cable construction. Keep the future-fiber pull box empty and accessible. No radius for this future cable is authorized by the installed control-cable schedule.

## 3.1 Final walkdown

Walk the station with the owner after restoration and commissioning. Check labels, access, guards, spare inventory, and missing records against the deficiency register. Close each deficiency with visible evidence or an accepted test result. Record any agreed deferred work with its owner and completion condition.

## 3.2 Spare filter screens

The spare ventilation intake-screen set quantity is 1. Package the set for dry storage and identify the intake assembly it fits. The screen set is separate from replacement supply-air filters and drive cooling filters. Enter the delivered set in the signed spare-part register.
