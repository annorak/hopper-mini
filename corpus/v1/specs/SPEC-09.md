# SPEC-09 Wastewater pumps and motors

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Pump arrangement

Provide matched centrifugal wastewater pumps for the retained wet well. The operating arrangement uses duty pumps and an installed standby pump under automatic alternation. The owner may isolate an individual branch while the remaining branches stay available. Coordinate pump removal with the access opening and lifting arrangement.

## 1.2 Duty flow

The design flow per duty pump is 32 L/s. Demonstrate that duty point on the certified pump curve with the supplied impeller. Submit the curve for the actual motor speed and installed hydraulic arrangement. The supplier shall identify the permitted operating region and the minimum continuous-flow limit.

## 2.1 Duty quantity

The simultaneous duty pump count is 2. Provide an additional installed standby pump of the same hydraulic selection. The control system rotates the lead assignment after a completed normal cycle. A failed pump shall drop out of rotation and raise a fault for the operator.

## 2.2 Pump warranty

The pump warranty period is 18 months. The period begins at station acceptance. Include the pump, motor, seal assembly, and supplier site service in the warranty. A repaired component shall return to service with the supplier documented inspection and the cause of the original failure.

## 2.3 Seals and cooling

Provide a cartridge seal arrangement suitable for intermittent wastewater pumping and the expected wet-well environment. Bring seal-leak and motor-temperature contacts to the local control panel. Do not use those contacts as a substitute for the motor protective relay. Identify the required cooling condition on the operating sheet.

## 3.1 Motor power

The rated power per duty pump motor is 30 kW. Select motors for the supplied drive and the station electrical service. The supplier shall state the permissible starts, cooling requirements, and protection settings for drive operation. Attach a durable rotation arrow before the motor enters site service.

## 3.2 Pump site duty test

Measure discharge flow and suction and discharge pressures with calibrated instruments during the site duty test. Record motor current at the same operating point. Investigate vibration or unstable flow before the reliability run. The supplier representative shall sign the final pump operating record after deficiencies are corrected.
