# SPEC-03 Earthwork and trench support

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Excavation limits

Excavate the discharge-main corridor from the wet-well wall to the existing connection chamber. Keep the retained gravity sewer in service and expose it by hand near the crossing. Stockpile acceptable material on the landward side of the trench. Maintain drainage away from the open excavation.

## 1.2 Disposal quantity

The estimated off-site excavation volume is 180 m3. This is a loose-volume haulage allowance for unsuitable trench spoil and foundation cut. Weighbridge tickets shall identify the receiving facility and the load date. Segregate clean excavated material from material that shows staining or odor before transport.

## 2.1 Pressure-main bedding

Bedding under the pressure main shall be washed angular aggregate. Place bedding across the full trench width and shape it to support the pipe barrel. Keep coupling recesses clear so the joint does not carry the pipe weight. Native excavated soil is not an acceptable bedding substitute.

## 2.2 Haulage allowance

The nominal loose-soil volume per haul truck is 12 m3. Use this value for the tender haul-count allowance. Actual legal truck limits and disposal receipts govern the transport work. Cover loads before they leave the site and keep soil off the access road.

## 2.3 Trench working width

The planned pressure-main trench width is 1.4 m. The width includes working space for joint assembly and compaction beside the pipe. Do not reduce the width at bends to avoid relocating temporary shoring. Maintain the support arrangement throughout pipe placement and initial backfill.

## 3.1 Unsuitable founding material

The engineer of record shall determine the overexcavation depth below the north foundation after the bearing surface is exposed. That depth is not specified in the contract documents. Stop excavation at the planned foundation level and obtain the engineer of record decision before removing additional founding material.

## 3.2 Backfill acceptance

Place backfill without disturbing line or grade, and compact beside the pipe before building cover above it. Test each distinct backfill material before use. The testing agency shall identify the lift and location on each density record. Rework failed areas before the next lift conceals them.
