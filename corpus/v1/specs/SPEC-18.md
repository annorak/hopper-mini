# SPEC-18 Environmental controls and odor treatment

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Site environmental controls

Keep sediment, fuel, and washwater out of the storm drainage system. Place spill materials near temporary pumps and the refueling point. Inspect containment after deliveries and after rain. Report a release to the owner immediately and retain the cleanup record with the site environmental log.

## 1.2 Odor control arrangement

Connect the wet-well extraction duct to the carbon odor unit without drawing air through the control room. Keep the unit access door clear for media replacement. Provide inlet and outlet sample points with permanent labels. The supplier shall state the accepted sampling method in the operating manual.

## 2.1 Carbon housing

Provide a corrosion-resistant carbon housing with sealed media access and a drained low point. Support the media without bypass paths around the bed. The unit shall permit media removal without dismantling the extraction fan. Protect the sample-point connections against accidental damage during media changes.

## 2.2 Odor breakthrough threshold

The hydrogen-sulfide breakthrough threshold for carbon-media replacement is not specified. The engineer of record shall set that threshold after the owner approves the odor monitoring plan. A supplier example value is not an authorized station setpoint. Do not populate the maintenance trigger with an assumed threshold.

## 2.3 Noise and exhaust

Direct temporary engine exhaust away from the control-room intake and the public path. Keep engine silencers installed during bypass operation. Coordinate unusually noisy work with the owner daily work notice. The contractor shall keep the gate closed when the site is unattended without blocking ventilation openings.

## 3.1 Environmental acceptance

Remove temporary containment only after the associated fuel and chemical stock leaves the site. Inspect storm inlets for construction sediment and clean affected areas. Verify odor duct seals during the extraction-fan test. Submit the disposal receipts for spent construction absorbents and removed contaminated material.

## 3.2 Media and waste records

Identify the initial carbon media lot in the operating manual. Give the owner handling and disposal information for spent media. Keep unused media sealed and dry until installation. Waste from the odor unit shall not enter the building sump or the wet well.
