# SPEC-14 Ventilation and room conditioning

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Ventilation scope

Provide separate ventilation for the control room and the process valve room. Keep moist process air out of the electrical equipment space. Position outdoor intakes away from the wet-well vent and vehicle exhaust route. Coordinate duct access with drive-panel doors and the pump removal path.

## 1.2 Control-room volume

The room-volume calculation uses the clear internal control-room dimensions. The room volume excludes the ceiling service void and the adjacent valve room. Use the resulting clear volume for the ideal air-replacement calculation. Equipment heat-load calculations remain part of the ventilation supplier submittal.

## 2.1 Control-room supply airflow

The scheduled control-room supply airflow is 20 m3/min. Balance the supply with the room in its normal operating configuration and the doors closed. Record airflow at the accepted filter condition. Provide accessible balancing devices so the owner can restore the accepted setting after maintenance.

## 2.2 Supply-air filter

Control-room supply-air filters shall be MERV 13. Use a gasketed filter rack that prevents air bypass around the filter frame. Mark the airflow direction on the housing. Provide access to withdraw the filter without moving the drive panel or disconnecting a duct section.

## 2.3 Ductwork and isolation

Support ductwork from the building structure and isolate fan vibration from rigid connections. Seal joints exposed to weather. Provide insect screens at outdoor openings without obstructing service access. Keep condensate from collecting in the outdoor intake section.

## 3.1 Ventilation proving

Verify fan rotation, supply airflow, filter differential indication, and alarm contacts. Test the fan-failure signal at the station controller. Record the final balancing position on each damper. Correct air paths that draw valve-room air into the control room before the drive-panel heat test.

## 3.2 Maintenance access

Label fan isolators at the access side of the equipment. Leave the supplier service envelope clear of cable trays and pipe supports. Give the owner replacement belt and filter references with the maintenance record. Provide a local means of reading the filter differential indication during routine rounds.
