# SPEC-16 Protective coatings and corrosion control

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Coating scope

Coat the new exposed steel discharge spools and repair damaged factory finishes on supports. Keep machined flange faces, nameplates, and valve stems free of coating. Use a coating system rated for the valve-room humidity and intermittent washdown. Isolate dissimilar metals at the support interfaces.

## 1.2 Application planning

The coating work area is the exposed steel spool area shown on the mechanical detail sheet. Use the stated coverage rate for a single application planning calculation. The required completed system thickness remains a separate acceptance criterion. Include waste and touch-up material as a contractor allowance outside that theoretical calculation.

## 2.1 Epoxy thickness

The completed epoxy dry-film thickness is 300 micrometres. Apply the supplier compatible primer and finish system over the accepted prepared surface. Measure the completed dry film at accessible locations on every spool. Correct low readings before installing insulation or other items that conceal the coating.

## 2.2 Theoretical coverage

The theoretical single-coat coverage rate is 8 m2/L. This rate applies to the specified coating product at the supplier application condition. It excludes overspray, container residue, and surface-profile losses. Keep batch and mixing records with the coating inspection report.

## 2.3 Surface preparation

Remove weld spatter, oil, and soluble contamination before abrasive preparation. Keep prepared steel dry and free of flash rust until coating. Round sharp edges enough to permit the coating to cover them. The coating inspector shall release each prepared area before application starts.

## 3.1 Inspection and repairs

Check ambient conditions, surface condition, and cure before handling coated spools. Repair transport damage with the same compatible system. Inspect bolt and support contact areas after final assembly. Keep repaired areas accessible until the owner accepts the repair record.

## 3.2 Corrosion handover

Supply product data, batch numbers, preparation records, thickness readings, and repair locations in the closeout package. Identify the coating touch-up kit by the same system name used in the installed work. The owner maintenance record shall distinguish field-applied coating from the retained factory finish.
