# SPEC-12 Field instrumentation

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Instrumentation scope

Provide wet-well level measurement, discharge pressure indication, flow measurement, and pump-condition contacts. Identify each instrument with the station tag used in the input-output schedule. Keep calibration access available without pump removal. Supply the instrument mounting and process isolation items with the instrument package.

## 1.2 Operating storage

The usable wet-well operating volume is 72 m3. This volume lies between the normal pump-control limits used for the operating-storage calculation. It does not establish the separate high-high alarm elevation. Verify the level-to-volume table with the retained wet-well dimensions before final controller setup.

## 2.1 Primary level technology

The primary wet-well level transmitter shall use radar sensing. Provide a noncontact device with a corrosion-resistant process antenna. Mount the transmitter clear of the incoming jet and the pump extraction path. The signal shall indicate a failed measurement rather than holding the last good value indefinitely.

## 2.2 Net filling condition

The design net wet-well filling rate is 0.024 m3/s. This value describes the inflow condition with the pumps stopped for the storage-time calculation. It is separate from the pump duty flow and temporary bypass design flow. Use the same volume units when preparing the operating-storage worksheet.

## 2.3 High-high alarm elevation

The wet-well high-high alarm elevation is not specified. The engineer of record shall set that elevation after the owner confirms the overflow risk assessment. Normal pump-control levels do not define this alarm setting. Leave the final high-high setpoint entry uncommitted until the engineer of record issues the value.

## 3.1 Cable allowance

The instrument cable service allowance is 15 m. Add this allowance to the measured cable route for the level-transmitter loop purchase quantity. Provide slack at the panel and instrument without leaving loose coils in the wet well. Label the cable before either end is terminated.

## 3.2 Calibration and loop checks

Calibrate each analog instrument with a traceable reference before its loop check. Record the applied input and indicated controller value across the working range. Verify loss-of-signal behavior at the station controller. Correct reversed scaling or tag mismatches before the site duty test begins.
