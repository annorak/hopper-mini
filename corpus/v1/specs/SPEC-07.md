# SPEC-07 Process valves and actuators

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Valve service

Provide isolation valves, nonreturn valves, and drain valves for the new pump discharge branches and common manifold. All process valves handle screened municipal wastewater. Keep the flow direction visible after installation. The valve schedule and shop drawings shall identify each valve by the station equipment tag.

## 1.2 Valve submittals

Submit valve body material, seat material, flange pattern, pressure rating, and actuator data in a coordinated package. Show the clearance required to withdraw the seat and remove the actuator. A valve shall not rely on removal of an adjacent permanent support for routine service.

## 2.1 Pump discharge isolation valve

The valve on each pump discharge shall be a butterfly valve. Use a resilient-seated body suitable for wastewater isolation. Locate the operator where the owner can reach it from the platform. Provide a visible position indicator that remains readable with the actuator cover installed.

## 2.2 Actuator control load

Each motorized valve actuator control load is 90 W. The load allowance covers actuator controls and indication during the operating state used for the panel load schedule. Provide local open and close controls with a lockable remote selection. Report an actuator stall through a separate alarm contact.

## 2.3 Check valves

Use a swing check valve with an external position indication on each pump branch. Orient the valve for the installed flow direction and keep the service cover accessible. The check valve shall close without striking adjacent pipework. Coordinate the access envelope with the dismantling joint.

## 3.1 Actuator quantity

The motorized valve actuator count is 6. The count includes pump-branch and manifold isolation duties in the valve schedule. Verify each tag against the control terminal schedule before wiring. Manual drain valves do not require an actuator or a remote position signal.

## 3.2 Valve site checks

Stroke each motorized valve through its full travel before wet testing. Verify local and remote indication at both ends of travel. Confirm that mechanical stops do not force the seat beyond the supplier setting. Record valve tag, direction of rotation, and final switch settings on the commissioning sheet.
