# SPEC-01 General requirements

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Work limits

The Bayview Pump Station Upgrade replaces the process pumps, discharge manifold, motor controls, and station ventilation while the collection system remains in service. The owner retains the existing wet well and the utility service. Work includes temporary pumping, civil restoration, commissioning, operator training, and the final operating record.

## 1.2 Order of precedence

The latest addendum overrides the affected specification section. Specifications govern conflicting drawing notes. Unresolved omissions require a decision from the engineer of record. An addendum changes only the requirement it names. Submit a request for information before procurement when a document conflict affects an equipment selection, and identify both source citations.

## 2.1 Submittal register

Submit a coordinated register that identifies the pump, valve, drive, instrument, and coating packages before purchase. Each package shall state the proposed manufacturer and model, the specified service, and every proposed deviation. Approval of a product data sheet does not approve a deviation hidden in vendor literature.

## 2.2 Field coordination

The contractor shall measure existing openings and pipe centerlines before fabrication. Include those measurements in the manifold shop drawing. Keep equipment removal paths open, and reserve the access apron for the owner during operating rounds. Report an inaccessible connection before demolition removes the only means of restoring service.

## 2.3 Owner access

The owner operates the station and controls all starts and stops of existing equipment. Provide a clear walking route from the road gate to the control room. Give the operator the daily isolation list before each shift. A contractor lock does not replace the owner lock on a shared isolation point.

## 3.1 Quality records

Keep inspection reports, pressure-test sheets, instrument calibration records, and deficiency notices in the site office. Record the equipment tag on every test sheet. Submit corrected results as revisions linked to the original failed test so the owner can distinguish a retest from a missing record.

## 3.2 Protection and restoration

Protect retained cables, drainage paths, and the wet-well vent during construction. Repair damage caused by the work with materials that match the adjoining retained construction. Complete restoration after heavy deliveries have ended. The owner may use completed equipment before acceptance without waiving outstanding deficiencies.
