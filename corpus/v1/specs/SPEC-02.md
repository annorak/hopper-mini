# SPEC-02 Construction sequence and temporary pumping

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Sequence of work

Install and prove temporary pumping before isolating the existing discharge main. Remove the existing manifold only after the operator confirms that the temporary system carries the incoming flow. Keep temporary pumping available until the permanent pumps pass their site duty test and the owner authorizes its removal.

## 1.2 Station outage

The maximum planned station outage is 6 hours. Give the owner a written isolation sequence before the outage. Include the rollback connection, the responsible operator, and the last safe time to abandon the tie-in. An outage begins when the existing permanent pumping path becomes unavailable.

## 2.1 Bypass duty

The temporary bypass design flow is 18 L/s. Provide automatic lead and standby units with separate suction arrangements. Route discharge hose clear of the public footpath and restrain each change in direction. The standby unit shall start on loss of the lead unit without a visit to the site.

## 2.2 Bypass operating allowance

The daily bypass pumping allowance is 28800 seconds. This allowance is for temporary fuel and discharge-volume planning. It is not a restriction on emergency operation. Keep a refueling procedure that permits pumping to continue, and record lead-pump run time at each operator round.

## 2.3 Temporary storage

The temporary storage tank gross volume is 96 m3. The required unused freeboard volume is 24 m3. Use a closed tank with a screened vent and a visible level indication. The tank does not replace bypass capacity, and its outlet shall not create an uncontrolled gravity return to the wet well.

## 3.1 Bypass proving test

Run the temporary system with clean water before connecting it to live sewage. Demonstrate automatic standby transfer, high-level alarm, and generator operation. Verify hose restraint and inspect every coupling during the run. Correct leakage before the owner permits permanent equipment isolation.

## 3.2 Demobilization

Flush temporary hoses into the process system under operator control. Capture residual liquid before transport, and cap hose ends. Remove temporary anchors that could obstruct maintenance vehicles. Restore ground disturbed by the temporary pumps after the permanent station completes its reliability run.
