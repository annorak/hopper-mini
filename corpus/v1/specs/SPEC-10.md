# SPEC-10 Electrical service and raceways

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Electrical work limits

Retain the utility metering cabinet and replace the station distribution downstream of the main isolation point. Provide new process feeders, grounding bonds, and raceways. Coordinate the service outage with the owner and utility. Identify retained energized circuits before cable removal begins.

## 1.2 Demand schedule

The electrical demand schedule distinguishes the control-building load from the process feeder allowance. Use the diversified values for the new distribution load summary. Verify panel identification against the owner switching diagram. A demand allowance does not reduce the required equipment nameplate rating or protective-device coordination.

## 2.1 Process demand

The process feeder demand allowance is 60 kW. This value is the process entry in the distribution load summary. Carry the control-building diversified demand as a separate entry. Submit the completed load summary with the distribution shop drawing before release for manufacture.

## 2.2 Wet-well exposed conduit

Exposed raceway in the wet well shall be PVC-coated rigid steel conduit. Seal cut coating edges with the raceway supplier repair compound. Keep joints accessible above the maintenance platform wherever the layout permits. Provide bonding continuity across threaded connections and separate process instrumentation from power conductors.

## 2.3 Feeder capacity

The new auxiliary feeder continuous-current capacity is 100 A. The capacity applies after the specified installation derating. Submit cable selection and protection settings with the feeder schedule. Verify the conductor termination rating before selecting a protective-device setting at the feeder limit.

## 3.1 Feeder load verification

Measure auxiliary feeder current after the permanent ventilation, drainage, and control loads are running. Record the operating configuration with the reading. Compare the measured load with the submitted load schedule. An unexplained load above the schedule requires investigation before the owner accepts the feeder.

## 3.2 Electrical acceptance

Test insulation resistance before connecting electronic equipment. Verify phase sequence at the distribution point and rotation at each motor under supplier supervision. Label both cable ends and each raceway termination. Submit the test record and the final protective-device settings before energizing permanent process equipment.
