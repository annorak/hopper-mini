# SPEC-19 Operation, maintenance, and operator instruction

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Operating manuals

Provide a station manual organized around operator tasks and equipment tags. Include normal start and stop, local isolation, response to common alarms, and recovery after power loss. Use the supplied equipment models and the accepted controller program. Remove supplier literature for options that are not installed.

## 1.2 Maintenance records

State the inspection interval, lubricant, wear limits, and replacement part reference for each maintained component. Identify work that requires an equipment isolation or an entry permit. Include pump seal and bearing records, valve service instructions, and the drive parameter recovery procedure. The manual shall match the final accepted equipment.

## 2.1 Operating procedures

Prepare normal operation and abnormal-operation procedures with the owner operator. Keep temporary bypass instructions in a separate construction record. The permanent procedure shall identify which local protections remain active during manual operation. Do not advise an operator to bypass a trip to keep a pump running.

## 2.2 Maintenance demonstration

Demonstrate removal of a grating panel, access to the valve service cover, and retrieval of the drive configuration file. Use the installed equipment during the demonstration. Identify special tools and isolation points before starting. Record access defects that prevent the owner from completing a routine maintenance task.

## 2.3 Training material

Prepare a station-specific training agenda and a copy of the accepted alarm list. Use drawings that show the final tags and connection routes. Include the remote-restart policy only after the engineer of record has issued the selection. Training shall not convert an unresolved design choice into an operating assumption.

## 3.1 Operator training duration

The required operator training duration is 6 hours. Deliver the training after the manuals and commissioning record are available. Include practical operation at the local interface and a guided review of the maintenance isolation points. Record attendance and the topics completed.

## 3.2 Training completion

Give the owner the attendance record, presentation files, and unanswered technical questions after training. Resolve incorrect manual content before issuing the final manual. The contractor shall arrange a focused follow-up session for material changed after the original instruction. Acceptance of attendance alone does not close equipment deficiencies.
