# SPEC-06 Structural metalwork and access

Bayview Pump Station Upgrade | Contract specification

## 1.1 Metalwork scope

Provide access grating, wet-well hatch framing, pipe support steel, and the removable guardrail at the pump extraction opening. Fabricate assemblies so the owner can remove a pump without cutting permanent steel. Repair damaged protective finish after erection and keep bolt threads free of concrete splatter.

## 1.2 Grating schedule

The scheduled total grating panel count is 36. The schedule includes new and retained panels at the manifold platform. Mark panel locations on the shop drawing so replacement panels return to their intended openings. Retained panels remain subject to the inspection requirements in the execution section.

## 2.1 Support fabrication

Use welded support stools for the discharge manifold and provide slotted leveling plates at the base. Shop-weld assemblies where transport permits. Do not weld to the retained pipework. The support arrangement shall allow access to dismantling-joint tie rods and valve flange bolts.

## 2.2 Wet-well hatch material

The wet-well access hatch cover shall be aluminum. Provide a corrosion-resistant hinge and a positive hold-open device. Isolate dissimilar metal fasteners from the hatch frame. The cover shall close without trapping the pump lifting cable or obstructing the wet-well vent.

## 2.3 Existing hoist beam

The allowable lifting load for reuse of the existing hoist beam is not established. The engineer of record shall determine that load after the beam and connections are inspected. Do not infer a lifting rating from the pump mass or the original paint marking. Keep the existing hoist out of service pending the decision.

## 3.1 Retained grating inspection

Inspect retained grating for cracked welds, section loss, and bent bearing bars after removal. Quarantine damaged panels for the engineer review. Clean the support ledges before reinstalling accepted panels. New panel fasteners shall remain captive when a panel is lifted for maintenance.

## 3.2 Erection checks

Check guardrail continuity, hatch operation, and panel seating before the owner uses the platform. Remove sharp edges and loose metal debris. Record final anchor torque with the support tag and installer name. Protect open platform edges whenever a grating panel is removed.
